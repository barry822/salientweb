SalientBot

SalientBot is Salient’s web crawler. If you found this page in your server logs, this is who visited your website, why, and how to ask us to stay away.

Who runs it

Salient, a B2B outbound and lead generation company in Bengaluru, India. You can reach us at sales@salientco.in.

What it does

It reads public pages of company websites to learn about the business: what it sells and to whom, where it is, roughly how large it is, the technologies on its site, and the jobs it is hiring for. We use this to judge which companies may be a good fit for the businesses we work with.

It reads a handful of pages per company: the home page, and pages such as about, team, contact and careers. It does not crawl whole websites.

How it behaves

  • It names itself in every request. Its user agent contains SalientBot and a link to this page.
  • It reads your robots.txt first and follows it, both the rules for SalientBot and the rules for all bots. It honours Crawl-delay.
  • It visits slowly: one visit to a website at a time, at least a minute between visits, and a few seconds between pages. If your site answers 429 or 503, it waits for your Retry-After. If it answers 401 or 403, it stays away for a week.
  • It never logs in, never fills in forms, never solves CAPTCHAs, and never disguises itself or uses proxies.
  • It reads only HTML and text, at most a few megabytes a page.

What we keep

  • Facts about the company, and the names and job titles of leaders that the website itself lists.
  • Shared company addresses such as info@ or sales@, never a person’s email address taken from a website.
  • A log of the pages visited (address, status and the first few hundred characters), kept for 90 days. We don’t store copies of your pages.

How to ask it to stay away

Add this to your website’s robots.txt. SalientBot checks it before every visit:

User-agent: SalientBot
Disallow: /

Or email sales@salientco.in with your domain. We add it to our do-not-visit list, and SalientBot won’t read it again.

Questions about the data we hold about your company, or a request to remove it: write to sales@salientco.in.
