FAQ

Questions, answered.

What does Salient do?

Salient is a managed B2B outbound service. We run your entire outbound function: building target-account lists, reaching decision-makers across cold calling, email and LinkedIn, qualifying real buying intent, and placing qualified sales conversations directly into your pipeline.

How is this different from hiring an SDR or building an in-house team?

You don't carry salaries, tooling, ramp time or attrition. Instead of hiring and managing headcount, you get qualified pipeline as an outcome, a fully operated outbound function that's live from week one, not three to six months after a new rep starts.

Which companies do you work with?

Any B2B company with a sales-led motion: software, IT services, fintech, logistics, professional services and more. If you need more qualified sales conversations but don't want to build the machinery to generate them, Salient is a fit.

Which outbound channels do you use?

Cold calling, email and LinkedIn at the core, with paid (Meta and Google) layered in as you scale. The same buyer research feeds every channel, so it runs as one coordinated campaign rather than disconnected silos.

How does pricing work?

We're built around outcomes rather than headcount or activity. You pay for qualified pipeline, not seats, and we offer both outcome-based and retainer models. We scope the commercials to your pilot on a short call, so pricing matches the volume and market you're targeting.

How do we get started?

We begin with a short scoping call to understand your ICP, then run a pilot that proves what converts before you commit to anything long-term. Discovery typically begins within days of signing.
